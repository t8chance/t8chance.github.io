This site's chance is the share of the forecast versions that reach the signal for that storm, from the forecast this site opens on: Google DeepMind's (Weather Lab cyclone forecast, 50 versions) where it had a track for the storm, otherwise ECMWF's physics forecast (about 50 versions). It is the newest run that was public when MET WARN posted (Google DeepMind about 8 hours after the run started, ECMWF about 13 hours), so the forecast was 8–18 hours older than the post. Each row names the forecast used. MET WARN posts some forecasts as pictures only, and those are marked. The sources are MET WARN's posts on its public channel (t.me/s/metwarn, read October 2026) and HKO's signal records.
